RAUM27 brings a unique blend of electronic and rock elements to the stage. As the touring drummer, I perform with a hybrid setup combining acoustic drums with Roland electronic pads, creating a massive sound that bridges genres.

## The Setup
Hybrid kit with Roland SPD-SX sampling pad, acoustic shells, and electronic triggers for seamless transitions between organic and synthetic textures.

## The Experience
RAUM27 shows are known for their intensity — smoke machines, dynamic lighting, and a wall of sound that keeps the crowd moving from start to finish.
