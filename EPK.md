## BIOGRAPHY

Onur Gürsoy is a Hamburg-based professional drummer best known as the rhythmic backbone for the chart-topping indie-pop duo **RAUM27**. Known for his high-energy live performances and meticulous technical execution, Onur has played a crucial role in translating studio productions into massive, stadium-ready live arrangements. From sweaty, packed club shows to commanding main stages at some of Germany’s largest festivals, his playing is defined by its pocket, power, and seamless integration of electronic and acoustic elements.

Beyond his primary role with RAUM27, Onur is a highly sought-after session and live drummer. His versatility allows him to adapt effortlessly across genres, serving as a live sub and session player for acts like **Chaos und Liebe**, **Useful**, **Remote Bondage**, **Nils Wandrey**, **shedancedslowly**, and **Vladi Wostock**. Whether tracking nuanced studio takes or anchoring a high-octane rock set, he brings a deep understanding of song structure, dynamics, and groove to every project.

What sets Onur apart in the modern music industry is his unique dual-career as an AI Engineer and Computer Scientist. This highly analytical background translates directly to his musical profession—making him an exceptionally structured musician who excels at managing complex playback rigs, programming hybrid drum setups (SPD-SX, triggers, MIDI), and ensuring absolute reliability and consistency on tour. He bridges the gap between raw musical emotion and technical perfection.
